# Zen Site Folders v0.9.1 — Consecutive background-tab drags

## Changes

- Keep the selection guard on a tab that is still landing when another tab is pressed.
- Match drag completion to its source so an earlier drag cannot end the next gesture.
- Activate a background tab on a completed click. A release without a click leaves the tab asleep.

## Compatibility / known limitations

- Zen and Zia handle placement, group animation, and Split View. Dropping into the page can still activate a split pane.
- The Sine background-drag preference keeps its existing default and stored value.

## Verification

- Regression coverage includes overlapping drop and press events, consecutive native reordering of discarded tabs, ordinary clicks, and Split View selection.

## Rollback

- Turn off “Drag background tabs without activating or loading them” in Sine to return to native selection behavior.
- For a full rollback, reinstall v0.9.0 through Sine. That version includes the previous background-drag behavior.
