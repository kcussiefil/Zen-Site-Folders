# Zen Site Folders v0.5.5

**Whole website-group dragging now uses Zia's actual single native drag controller.**

On native dragstart, the grabbed ordinary site group is transiently recognized by Zia as a folder. Zia alone then owns the live displaced rows, visual position, and final drop. Site Folders does NOT draw its second card or run its second order correction. The adapter restores the original group identity and any transient Zen method wrapper after the gesture. Prevents nested site-folder drops, preserving top-level grouping and SessionStore snapshots. Without Zia or a native dragstart, the previously verified pointer-only fallback remains. Sine timestamps, vertical edge scroll and compact-mode hover previews remain unchanged.

CI includes direct Zia takeover, non-nesting and cleanup tests; physical macOS gesture testing remains necessary.
