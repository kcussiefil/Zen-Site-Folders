# Zen Site Folders v0.5.6

Fixes two v0.5.5 drag regressions reported in the user's screen recording.

- Native member-tab dragstart now immediately removes the pointer fallback source hiding, card and scroll loop. It no longer waits for another dragover before showing the native tab.
- Ordinary site groups now land in their original unpinned container during Zia's own insertion, before Zia measures its landing animation. The destination comes from Zia's painted row shifts. The previous delayed second reorder has been removed.
- The scoped separator insertion adapter is restored after the gesture. It does not modify upstream Zia, replace tabs or change group membership.
- Preview sidebar hold, glow styling, collapse, Sine updates, safe splitting and restoration remain intact.

Regression coverage checks native tab visibility during scroll and records every group insertion for upward and downward drops, rejecting even temporary placement in the pinned container or multiple landings. Native tests run in disposable headless Zen profiles on Linux and macOS; physical macOS GUI/trackpad acceptance remains unverified.
