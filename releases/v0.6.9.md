# Zen Site Folders v0.6.9

Fixes persistent tab-opening time without changing the existing hover card layout, text, widths, spacing or time format.

- A new tab or native duplicated tab receives its own real opening timestamp, independent of its sort-order timestamp.
- Restored and lazily loaded tabs preserve previously recorded opening timestamps through Zen restart, workspace switching, grouping, tab refresh and wake-up; they are never loaded solely for the timer.
- A historical tab with no reliable opening timestamp continues to show `—`, rather than an invented date.
- SessionStore restoration events reconcile saved timestamps without adding polling, rescanning, or changes to group drag/collapse/animation.

Verification: event-driven Node regression tests and existing suite. UI remains identical to v0.6.8.
